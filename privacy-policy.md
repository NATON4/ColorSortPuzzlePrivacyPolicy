# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Developer: Anton Hryhorenko  
App: Color Sort Puzzle  
Package name: `com.antonhryhorenko.colorsortpuzzle`  
Contact: anthonygri.net@gmail.com

## Overview

Color Sort Puzzle is a casual mobile puzzle game. The app does not require users to create an account, does not allow users to log in, and does not ask users to provide profile information.

The app uses Google and Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, consent management, and advertising. These services may collect and process app activity, diagnostics, crash logs, and device or advertising identifiers as described below.

## Data We Collect Or Share

### App Activity

We may collect gameplay and app activity events, such as:

- level started and completed;
- selected game mode;
- moves, stars, hints, undo usage, and power-up usage;
- ad request, ad impression, ad reward, and ad availability events;
- app settings or configuration events.

This data is used for analytics, gameplay balancing, product improvement, and understanding how the app is used.

### Crash Logs And Diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics and related Firebase services to collect crash logs and diagnostic data. This may include:

- crash logs and stack traces;
- app version;
- device model;
- operating system version;
- technical diagnostic data related to app stability and performance.

This data is used to detect, diagnose, and fix bugs and crashes.

### Device Or Other IDs

Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and related Google services may collect device identifiers or advertising identifiers. These identifiers may be used for:

- analytics and measurement;
- ad delivery and ad performance measurement;
- fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

### Advertising Data

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob and related Google services may collect information such as ad impressions, ad interactions, device or advertising identifiers, and diagnostic information needed to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

### Remote Configuration

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust app behavior, such as ad settings and feature configuration. Remote Config may process app instance or device-related technical data as part of Firebase services.

### Local Game Progress

The app stores game progress and settings locally on the user's device. This may include unlocked levels, stars, completed levels, selected theme, audio settings, haptics settings, and other gameplay preferences.

Local progress is stored on the device and is not used to identify the user. Users can remove local progress by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## Data We Do Not Collect Directly

The app does not ask users to provide:

- name;
- email address;
- phone number;
- precise or approximate location;
- payment information;
- contacts;
- photos, videos, audio, files, or documents;
- messages;
- calendar events;
- account credentials.

The app does not include chat, user-generated content, account registration, or account login.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

- Google AdMob;
- Google User Messaging Platform;
- Firebase Analytics;
- Firebase Crashlytics;
- Firebase Remote Config.

These services are provided by Google. They may collect, process, and share data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Useful links:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Google AdMob: <https://admob.google.com/>
- Firebase Privacy and Security: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## How We Use Data

We use collected data to:

- provide and improve the game;
- understand app usage in aggregate;
- balance levels and improve gameplay;
- diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues;
- provide, measure, and improve ads;
- prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid ad activity;
- configure app behavior through Remote Config.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Data may be shared with Google, Firebase, AdMob, and related service providers when needed for analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, remote configuration, advertising, security, fraud prevention, and compliance.

## Data Retention

Local game progress remains on the user's device until the user clears app data or uninstalls the app.

Analytics, advertising, crash, and diagnostic data are retained by Google/Firebase/AdMob according to their own retention practices and applicable settings.

## User Choices

Users can:

- manage ad consent and privacy options in the app where Google consent controls are required;
- clear local game progress by clearing app data or uninstalling the app;
- adjust app settings such as audio and haptics;
- contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Some data collection by Google services is required for analytics, crash reporting, advertising, security, fraud prevention, and app operation. If a user does not want this data processed, they should stop using the app and uninstall it.

## Children's Privacy

Color Sort Puzzle is not directed to children. The app is intended for a general adult audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## International Data Transfers

Google, Firebase, and AdMob may process data on servers located in different countries. Data may therefore be transferred and processed outside the user's country of residence.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its services change. The updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.
