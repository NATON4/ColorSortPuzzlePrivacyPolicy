## Overview

Color Sort Puzzle is a casual mobile puzzle game. The app does not require users to create an account, does not allow users to log in, and does not ask users to provide personal profile information.

The app uses Google and Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, consent management, and advertising. These services may collect and process app activity, diagnostics, crash logs, and device or advertising identifiers as described below.

---

## Data We Collect Or Share

### App Activity

We may collect gameplay and app activity events, such as:

- level started and completed;
- selected game mode;
- moves, stars, hints, undo usage, and power-up usage;
- ad request, ad impression, ad reward, and ad availability events;
- app settings or configuration events.

This data is used for analytics, gameplay balancing, product improvement, and understanding how the app is used.

### Crash Logs And Diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics and related Firebase services to collect crash logs and diagnostic data. This may include:

- crash logs and stack traces;
- app version;
- device model;
- operating system version;
- technical diagnostic data related to app stability and performance.

This data is used to detect, diagnose, and fix bugs and crashes.

### Device Or Other IDs

Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and related Google services may collect device identifiers or advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID on Android). These identifiers may be used for:

- analytics and measurement;
- ad delivery and ad performance measurement;
- fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

### Advertising Data

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Where the user has given consent, AdMob may show personalized ads based on interests and past interactions. Where consent has not been given or is not required, AdMob will show non-personalized ads.

AdMob and related Google services may collect information such as ad impressions, ad interactions, device or advertising identifiers, and diagnostic information needed to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services.

Where required by applicable law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices before any personalized advertising data is processed.

### Remote Configuration

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust app behavior, such as ad settings and feature configuration. Remote Config may process app instance or device-related technical data as part of Firebase services.

### Local Game Progress

The app stores game progress and settings locally on the user's device using the device's local storage. This may include unlocked levels, stars, completed levels, selected theme, audio settings, haptics settings, and other gameplay preferences.

Local progress is stored only on the device and is not transmitted to us. Users can remove local progress by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## User Choices

Users can:

- **Manage ad consent:** In jurisdictions where consent is required, users are shown a consent dialog when launching the app. Privacy choices can be reviewed and updated through any in-app privacy settings provided by the Google User Messaging Platform.
- **Opt out of personalized ads:** Users can opt out of interest-based advertising through their device settings (Google Settings → Ads on Android; or Settings → Privacy → Tracking on iOS).
- **Reset or delete advertising ID:** Users can reset their advertising identifier or opt out of ad tracking through their device's privacy settings.
- **Clear local progress:** Users can clear all locally stored game data by clearing the app's data in device settings or by uninstalling the app.
- **Request data access or deletion:** Users can contact us at anthonygri.net@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we may hold.
- **Stop data processing:** If a user does not want data processed by Google/Firebase/AdMob services, they should stop using the app and uninstall it.
